# Ember launcher

Prompt:

> A thrust, then an X of two diagonal cuts, a gold rising launcher, and a red air slam to
> finish. Rhythm: slow, fast fast, medium, heavy. Fire colours, bold blades.

How it was mapped: thrust = flat crescent (squash 0.18) with a lunge; the X = two diagonal
crescents with opposite tilts, 0.16 s apart; launcher = gold rising crescent with
`launch` −640; slam = the attacker jumps, red overhead crescent, `launch` 950 and
`finisher`. Fire colours = the `ember` preset; bold = thickness 1.4. 5 hits.
